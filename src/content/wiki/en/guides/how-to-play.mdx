## How do you launch it on a computer?

Use the normal Roblox Player launch flow described in [Roblox's installation help](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/204473560-How-to-Install-and-Play-Roblox):

1. Open the verified experience page and sign in to Roblox.
2. Select **Play**.
3. If Roblox Player is missing, follow Roblox's installation prompt.
4. If your browser asks which application should open the game link, choose Roblox.

These are Roblox's platform instructions. A successful join with a particular account or device has not been tested for this wiki. If access is unavailable, read the message on the current listing and consult the [developer community links](/resources/community-links/).

## What does the game involve?

The [official description](https://www.roblox.com/games/111838814545019/Morioh-High) presents a school life game set in Morioh, Japan. It describes classes, relationships, groups, exploration, and NPC interaction. It does not document exact NPC locations, quest rewards, combat keys, or a Stand acquisition route.
